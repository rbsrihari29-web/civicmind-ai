### Section 1: Stormwater Drainage Network Overview
The Greater Chennai Corporation maintains an urban stormwater drain network spanning 3,070 km across the four major river basins: Adyar River Basin, Cooum River Basin, Kosasthalaiyar River Basin, and Kovalam River Basin. Integrated stormwater drainage networks funded under the World Bank and KfW schemes are active in Thiruvottiyur, Manali, Madhavaram, Ambattur, Valasaravakkam, Alandur, and Perungudi.

### Section 2: Monsoon Hotspot Categorization & Deployment Rules
Vulnerable urban waterlogging locations are categorized into three operational risk tiers:
- **Tier 1 (Severe Inundation Risk):** Low-lying micro-catchments with recurring water stagnation depth exceeding 1.5 feet (e.g., Velachery AGS Colony, Madipakkam Kubera Nagar, Vyasarpadi Subways, Ganesapuram Subway, Pulianthope).
  * *Deployment Rule:* Pre-positioning of heavy 100 HP submersible diesel dewatering pumps and dedicated 24x7 generator backup 48 hours prior to forecasted depression / cyclonic landfall.
- **Tier 2 (Moderate Road Stagnation):** Arterial road dips and bus route roads where water accumulation ranges between 0.5 to 1.5 feet.
  * *Deployment Rule:* Mobile tractor-mounted 50 HP pumps dispatched within 2 hours of citizen alert.
- **Tier 3 (Localized Infiltration Delay):** Interior streets where water clears naturally within 2-4 hours of rain stoppage.
  * *Deployment Rule:* Clearing of silt catch-pit gratings by ward monsoon quick response teams (QRTs).

---

### Section 3: Subway & Arterial Road Traffic Inundation Protocols
Under Chennai Traffic Police (CTP) joint coordination protocols:
1. When water depth in vehicular subways (e.g. RBI Subway, Duraisamy Subway, Rangarajapuram Two-Wheeler Subway) reaches 1.0 foot, automatic water sensor barriers will engage, and traffic must be formally diverted.
2. The central monitoring desk at the Ripon Building Integrated Command and Control Centre (ICCC) monitors CCTV water gauge markers continuously.
3. Dewatering teams must operate continuously until the carriageway is clear of surface water and safe for motorized transit.
