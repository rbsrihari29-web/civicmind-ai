## URBAN PUBLIC INFRASTRUCTURE SAFETY & HAZARD ABATEMENT PROTOCOL

### Section 1: Scope & Critical Hazard Definitions
This protocol mandates standard operating procedures for detecting, barricading, and rectifying structural hazards on urban municipal thoroughfares, pedestrian pathways, utility networks, and bridges:
1. **Open or Fractured Manholes / Drain Chambers:** Any uncovered underground drainage or stormwater chamber creates an immediate life-safety hazard, especially during low visibility or rain.
2. **Damaged / Dangling Street Furniture & Utility Poles:** Electric poles tilted by impact or rusted at base level, hanging electrical cables with compromised insulation, or broken metal signposts.
3. **Collapsed Footpaths & Missing Kerb Stones:** Broken pedestrian sidewalks, open utility slits, or damaged tactile paving obstructing mobility for elderly citizens and persons with disabilities.
4. **Fallen Trees & Severed Limbs:** Trees with root failure or high-risk boughs resting on overhead electrical transmission wires or blocking fire-tender and emergency access routes.

---

### Section 2: Rapid Response & Safety Barricading SLAs
When a hazard alert is received:
- **Phase 1: Immediate Safety Isolation (Within 1 to 2 Hours):**
  * The designated Ward Assistant Engineer (AE) must immediately deploy reflective traffic cones, hazard caution tape, and physical barricades around any open manhole, sinkhole, or fallen live wire.
  * For dangling electric cables, the AE must instantly notify the local TANGEDCO section office to isolate the circuit breaker.
- **Phase 2: Emergency Tree Obstruction Clearing (Within 4 to 6 Hours):**
  * The Parks & Greenery squad equipped with mechanized chainsaws and cranes must cut and clear the trunk from the carriageway to restore traffic flow, followed by complete root ball extraction within 24 hours.
- **Phase 3: Permanent Infrastructure Restoration (Within 48 Hours to 5 Days):**
  * Manhole cover replacement with heavy-duty ductile iron or reinforced concrete pre-cast slabs: Within **6 hours**.
  * Streetlight fixture or ballast replacement: Within **48 hours**.
  * Paved footpath reconstruction: Within **5 working days**.

---

### Section 3: Legal Liability & Citizen Compensation Provisions
Under municipal tort law and public safety doctrine, municipal bodies bear an affirmative duty of care to ensure public ways are reasonably safe for ordinary traffic. Failure to barricade a known hazardous opening or delay in clearing an emergency obstruction beyond statutory timelines renders the administration liable for administrative inquiry and departmental disciplinary action under the Tamil Nadu Civil Services (Discipline and Appeal) Rules.
