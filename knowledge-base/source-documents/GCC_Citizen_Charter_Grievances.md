### Section 1: Overview & Jurisdiction
The Greater Chennai Corporation encompasses 15 administrative zones (Zones 1 to 15, from Thiruvottiyur to Sholinganallur) covering 200 electoral wards. This Citizen Charter establishes enforceable service level agreements (SLAs), standardized grievance escalation procedures, and citizen rights regarding public works, civic hygiene, and urban infrastructure maintenance.

---

### Section 2: Civic Grievance Resolution Timelines (Statutory SLAs)
Municipal field engineers and ward officers are bound by the following resolution timelines from the moment a complaint registration receipt number is issued:

1. **Potholes and Road Surface Craters:**
   - Bus Route Roads (BRR): Mandatory cold-mix or mastic asphalt filling within **48 hours**.
   - Interior / Ward Roads: Mandatory patching within **72 hours**.
   - Emergency sinkholes or collapsed culverts: Immediate hazard barricading within **2 hours**; structural restoration within **48 hours**.

2. **Municipal Solid Waste & Garbage Accumulation:**
   - Overflowing public compactor bins or roadside garbage mounds: Clearance within **24 hours**.
   - Dead animal removal from roadways: Urgent clearance within **6 hours**.
   - Construction and Demolition (C&D) waste dumping: Notice to violator within **24 hours**; physical removal within **48 hours** with debited clearance penalty.

3. **Waterlogging, Stormwater Drain Clogging & Flooding:**
   - Monsoon stormwater drain inlet desilting: Within **24 hours** of stagnation report.
   - Deploying high-discharge submersed diesel pumps at identified inundation junctions: Within **4 hours**.
   - Removal of road water pools obstructing vehicular traffic: Within **12 hours**.

4. **Damaged Public Infrastructure & Streetlights:**
   - Non-functioning or shattered streetlights: Restoration within **48 hours**.
   - Open, broken, or dislodged stormwater drain manhole covers: Immediate safety cordon/warning cone within **1 hour**; permanent pre-cast slab replacement within **6 hours**.
   - Damaged pedestrian footpaths and broken tactile pavers: Repair within **5 working days**.

5. **Fallen Trees and Road Obstructions:**
   - Fallen tree uprooted on arterial road or bus route: Immediate tree-pruning hydraulic squad dispatch within **2 hours**; thorough obstruction clearance within **6 hours**.
   - Fallen boughs on interior colony streets: Clearance within **12 hours**.

### Section 3: Official Reporting Channels & Verification
Citizens lodging grievances must note:
- **Central 24x7 Helpline:** Dial 1913 (Toll Free across all 15 GCC Zones).
- **Online Grievance Portal:** https://erp.chennaicorporation.gov.in/
- **GCC Namma Chennai Citizen Mobile Application:** Available for Android and iOS.
- **Physical Representation:** Zonal Office Public Grievance Day, conducted every Wednesday between 10:00 AM and 1:00 PM at the respective Zonal Office.

---

### Section 4: Mandatory Information Required in Official Complaints
To avoid administrative delays or rejection under Section 12(b), citizen submissions must include:
1. Precise geographic location: Specific Street Name, Ward Number, Zone Number, and closest prominent landmark.
2. Photographic evidence displaying both the defect in detail and wide-angle perspective indicating surroundings.
3. Physical dimensions or severity hazard level: Whether the defect entirely obstructs lane traffic or endangers school/hospital transit.
4. Contact details of the complainant for physical verification by the Assistant Engineer (AE - Works).

---

### Section 5: Grievance Escalation Hierarchy
If a registered complaint is not addressed within the statutory SLA:
- **Level 1 Escalation:** Assistant Executive Engineer (AEE) of the Sub-division.
- **Level 2 Escalation:** Executive Engineer (EE - Works) of the Administrative Zone.
- **Level 3 Escalation:** Regional Deputy Commissioner (RDC - North / Central / South).
- **Apex Oversight:** Principal Secretary / Commissioner, Greater Chennai Corporation.
