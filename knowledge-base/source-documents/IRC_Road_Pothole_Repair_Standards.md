### Section 1: Classification of Road Surface Defects
Road defects are classified under technical engineering standards to govern material and urgency allocation:
1. **Potholes (Severity Level P1 to P3):**
   - *P1 (Minor):* Depth < 25 mm, surface area < 0.1 m². Action: Regular maintenance cycle.
   - *P2 (Moderate):* Depth 25 to 50 mm, area 0.1 to 0.5 m². Action: Patching within 48 hours.
   - *P3 (Severe):* Depth > 50 mm or exposed aggregate/sub-base. Action: Emergency reinstatement within 24 hours to prevent vehicular suspension damage and two-wheeler fatal skidding.
2. **Alligator / Fatigue Cracking:** Interconnected polygonal cracking resulting from subgrade moisture saturation and repeated heavy axle loading.
3. **Utility Trench Settlement:** Depressions caused by improper backfilling and inadequate compaction after CMWSSB water/sewer line or TANGEDCO power cable trenching.

---

### Section 2: Approved Repair Methods
Under IRC:82 standards, the following repair specifications are mandatory:
- **Dry Season Patching:** Rectangular squaring of pothole perimeter, clearing loose dust/water with compressed air, applying Bitumen Emulsion RS-1 tack coat at 0.25 kg/m², filling with Dense Bituminous Macadam (DBM) or Bituminous Concrete (BC), and compacting using a 1-tonne vibratory roller or pneumatic tamper until no tire indentation is visible.
- **Monsoon Emergency Patching:** Use of certified Ready-To-Use Cold Mix Bituminous Pothole Patching Mix (IRC:SP:100). The mix contains anti-stripping additives and polymer-modified bitumen allowing immediate application directly onto damp or wet potholes, compacted under passing vehicular traffic or hand tampers.
- **Prohibited Practices:** Dumping raw construction debris, broken brickbats, or loose uncompacted gravel into potholes is strictly prohibited as it creates loose flying missiles and exacerbates surface erosion.

---

### Section 3: Utility Road Cutting Reinstatement Regulations
Under GCC Resolution No. 412/2021:
- No utility company or private contractor may cut roads without obtaining prior Road Cut Permission (RCP) from the Superintending Engineer (BRR).
- Following pipeline laying, the agency must compact granular sub-base (GSB) and Wet Mix Macadam (WMM) in 150 mm layers.
- The top wearing course must be laid by the Corporation works department using collected restoration charges within 7 days of trench handover.
