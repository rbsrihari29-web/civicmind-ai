### Section 1: Statutory Mandate & Source Segregation Duties
Under the Tamil Nadu Combined Development and Building Rules and the Solid Waste Management Rules, all domestic, commercial, and institutional waste generators within the Corporation limits must adhere to mandatory 3-way source segregation:
1. **Biodegradable / Wet Waste (Green Bin):** Organic kitchen waste, vegetable peelings, leftover food, garden dry leaves.
2. **Non-Biodegradable / Dry Recyclable Waste (Blue Bin):** Paper, cardboard, plastics, glass, metals, multi-layered packaging.
3. **Domestic Hazardous Waste (Red Bin / Wrap):** Expired pharmaceuticals, sanitary pads/diapers, fluorescent lamps, used batteries, chemical containers.

Door-to-door sanitary workers (Urbaser Sumeet in designated southern/central zones and Corporation sanitation personnel in north zones) collect segregated wet waste daily and dry recyclables on designated bi-weekly cycles.

### Section 2: Prohibited Acts & Penalties for Open Dumping
The following acts constitute cognizable municipal offenses subject to spot fines issued by Ward Sanitary Inspectors:
- **Open dumping on streets, vacant plots, or water channels:** First offense: INR 500; Repeated offense: INR 2,000.
- **Burning of municipal solid waste, leaf litter, or plastic residues:** Minimum fine of INR 5,000 under National Green Tribunal (NGT) directives.
- **Unsegregated waste handover by commercial bulk waste generators (>100 kg/day):** Spot penalty of INR 5,000; continued non-compliance triggers suspension of municipal trade license.
- **Dumping Construction and Demolition (C&D) debris on road shoulders or footpaths:** Spot fine of INR 2,000 for light vehicles and INR 5,000 per truckload. The violator must bear all disposal hauling costs to designated crushing yards at Kodungaiyur or Perungudi.

---

### Section 3: Commercial Establishments & Bulk Waste Generators
All marriage halls, hotels, shopping complexes, and educational institutions generating over 100 kg of municipal waste per day are legally categorized as Bulk Waste Generators (BWGs). Under Section 7 of the Bylaws, BWGs must install on-site composting units or contract authorized bio-methanation vendors. Handover of unprocessed bulk garbage to regular street collection bins is strictly prohibited.

---

### Section 4: Public Complaint Registration for Waste Clearance Failures
When a neighborhood street bin or transit dump point remains uncleared beyond 24 hours:
1. Citizens may submit geo-tagged photographic reports through the CivicMind platform or the 1913 municipal helpline.
2. The Sanitary Supervisor (SS) for the ward must acknowledge and deploy a mechanized tipper or compactor truck within 6 hours of formal notification.
3. If unaddressed, the matter escalates directly to the Zonal Health Officer (ZHO) and the Chief Engineer (Solid Waste Management).
